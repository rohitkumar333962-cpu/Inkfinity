Aftercare & FAQ

Does it hurt?

Some spots hurt more (ribs, spine, feet). Most people describe it as a steady scratching feeling. Eat well, sleep, and we'll pause whenever you ask.

How do I look after a new tattoo?

Keep the wrap on for the time your artist says, wash gently with mild soap, pat dry, apply a thin layer of recommended ointment. Avoid swimming, gym sweat, sun and picking scabs for about two weeks.

Is it safe?

Yes. We use sealed single-use needles and cartridges, disposable gloves and barriers, and disinfect every surface between clients.

Minimum age?

You must be 18 or older with a valid photo ID.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Our artists will adjust it so it works on skin and lasts well.

How long does healing take?

Surface healing takes 2 to 3 weeks. Full settling takes about 2 months.
